Research Statement

My research interests lie at the intersection of Mathematical Optimization and Large Language Models (LLMs). I am particularly fascinated by the role of Convex and Non-Convex Optimization in enhancing the efficiency and convergence of transformer-based architectures. My current work explores how structural constraints and principled optimization techniques can improve the reliability of machine learning models in low-resource environments.

Having built a rigorous foundation in Linear Algebra, Probability, and Discrete Structures at Sharif University, I seek to apply these theoretical insights to solve complex challenges in Computer Engineering—specifically in hardware-aware machine learning and the scalability of AI systems. I am eager to contribute to CUHK’s innovative research environment by developing optimization-centric solutions that push the boundaries of AI interpretability and performance.
